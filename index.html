How to read this

Your level is the highest one where you answered at least 60% correctly, provided you passed every level below it. This test measures grammar, vocabulary and reading. It does not assess listening, speaking or writing, so treat it as a solid estimate rather than an official certificate. For a formal result, see DELE or SIELE.
